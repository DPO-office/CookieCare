DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (CONTROLLED DEDUP FIXTURE)

1. Definitions. "Processor" means Vendor; "Controller" means Customer. "Personal Data" has the meaning given under applicable data protection law.

2. Processing. Processor shall process Personal Data only to provide the Services.

3. Sub-processors. Processor may appoint new sub-processors at any time without prior notice to, or prior consent from, Controller. Controller shall have no right to object to any sub-processor appointed by Processor.

4. Security Incidents. In the event of a security breach affecting Personal Data, Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay. No fixed time period for such notification is specified in this Agreement.

5. Deletion. Upon termination, Processor shall delete Personal Data within a commercially reasonable period.

6. Liability. Processor's liability under this Agreement is unlimited, and Processor shall indemnify Controller for any and all losses without cap or limitation of any kind.

7. Audit. Controller may audit Processor once per year at Controller's own expense.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands, and the courts of the Cayman Islands shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

ANNEX A - SUB-PROCESSING TERMS
A.1 For the avoidance of doubt, Processor may engage sub-processors at any time without prior notice to, or prior consent from, Controller, and Controller waives any right to object to such sub-processors.

ANNEX B - SECURITY MEASURES
B.1 Breach notification: Processor will inform Controller of any Personal Data breach without undue delay; the parties acknowledge that no specific deadline (such as 72 hours) applies under this Agreement.
