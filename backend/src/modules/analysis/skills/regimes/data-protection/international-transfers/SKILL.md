# EU SCCs / Schrems II operational overlay (draft)

## Coverage
Operational overlay for Commission Decision (EU) 2021/914 SCC modules, docking, transfer impact assessments, and EDPB Schrems II supplementary measures. GDPR Chapter V (Articles 44–49) remains in `regimes/data-protection/gdpr` and is not duplicated here.

## clause:international_transfer_mechanism
Mechanism for cross-border transfers (structural). Inventory this clause family first: destination, transfer tool (EU SCC / UK Addendum / BCR / adequacy / local mechanism), legal basis, supplementary measures, and cross-references to annexes or other transfer documents. Do not treat inventory as a Chapter V compliance verdict.

## rule:transfers.scc_module_selection
Identify the applicable 2021 SCC module (1 C2C, 2 C2P, 3 P2P, 4 P2C) and complete annexes. An unsigned or unmoduled pack is not a completed tool.

## rule:transfers.scc_docking
Where additional parties may join, keep the docking clause usable without rewriting the SCCs except to select modules or complete annexes.

## rule:transfers.tia_documented
Document a transfer impact assessment of destination-country law and practice before relying on SCCs (EDPB Recs 01/2020).

## rule:transfers.supplementary_measures
If the TIA shows local law prevents SCC compliance, adopt supplementary technical, contractual, or organisational measures, or do not transfer (EDPB Recs 01/2020 and 02/2020).

## risk:scc_module_gap
EU SCCs are present without a selected module or completed annexes.

## risk:scc_docking_gap
Additional parties cannot join the SCCs through the docking clause.

## risk:tia_missing
SCCs are relied on without a documented transfer impact assessment.

## risk:supplementary_measures_gap
Destination-country law may undermine the SCCs and no supplementary measures are documented.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
