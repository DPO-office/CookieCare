# CCPA / CPRA service-provider overlay (draft)

## Coverage
Primary source is the 20 March 2025 CCPA/CPRA Service Provider Contract. The small CCPA PDF in the skills folder is a 2018 law-review article and is not treated as the Act. This pack covers private business / service-provider contract duties and consumer-request assistance, not AG enforcement procedure.

## clause:data_protection
Core processing subject-matter, roles, and processor obligations annex.

## clause:data_subject_request_handling
Intake, identity checks, deadlines, decisions, and communications for rights requests.

## clause:security_dpia_assistance
Security / DPIA assistance language as a structural section.

## clause:subprocessor_flow_down
Subprocessor list / flow-down of processor obligations.

## rule:ccpa.sp.no_sell_share
The service provider must not sell or share personal information processed for the business, including for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## rule:ccpa.sp.business_purpose_limit
Retain, use, and disclose PI only for the specified business purpose, not for the provider's own purposes outside the direct business relationship (authorised subcontractors excepted).

## rule:ccpa.sp.no_combine
Do not combine client PI with other datasets except to perform a CPRA-permitted business purpose required by the client.

## rule:ccpa.sp.consumer_rights_assist
Assist with verifiable consumer requests (delete, know/access, correct). Forward requests received directly from consumers to the business.

## rule:ccpa.sp.toms
Implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the PI; do not lower the documented security level without recording substantial changes.

## risk:ccpa_sell_share_gap
The service provider is not barred from selling or sharing personal information.

## risk:ccpa_purpose_limit_gap
The provider may use personal information beyond the business purpose.

## risk:ccpa_combine_gap
The provider may combine client PI with other datasets without a CPRA-permitted purpose.

## risk:ccpa_consumer_rights_gap
The provider does not assist with verifiable consumer requests or may answer them directly.

## risk:ccpa_toms_gap
No appropriate technical and organisational measures for personal information.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
