# HIPAA business associate agreement skill (draft)

## Coverage
Operational BAA clauses from the UCLA Health HIPAA BAA (permitted uses, minimum necessary, safeguards, subcontractor flow-down, breach notice, return/destruction). The HHS PDF in the skills folder is a Katrina enforcement bulletin and is not used as a BAA source. Covered-entity institutional / OCR process duties are excluded.

## clause:phi_use_disclosure
Permitted uses and disclosures of protected health information.

## clause:hipaa_safeguards
Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for PHI / ePHI.

## clause:breach_notification
Notice to the covered entity of a Breach or Security Incident.

## clause:subprocessor_flow_down
Subprocessor list / flow-down of processor obligations.

## clause:deletion_on_termination
Return or deletion of personal data on termination.

## rule:hipaa.baa.permitted_uses
The business associate may use or disclose PHI only as permitted by the BAA or required by law.

## rule:hipaa.baa.minimum_necessary
Limit PHI use, access, and disclosure to the minimum necessary to perform the services.

## rule:hipaa.baa.safeguards
Implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for ePHI.

## rule:hipaa.baa.subcontractor_flowdown
Bind agents/subcontractors that handle PHI to written restrictions at least as protective as the BAA.

## rule:hipaa.baa.breach_notice
Notify the covered entity of a Breach or Security Incident in writing without unreasonable delay (UCLA source: as soon as possible, not more than two business days for specified incidents).

## rule:hipaa.baa.return_or_destroy
Return or destroy PHI at termination, with a documented infeasibility exception and continuing protections.

## risk:hipaa_permitted_uses_gap
The business associate may use or disclose PHI beyond the BAA or legal requirement.

## risk:hipaa_minimum_necessary_gap
PHI use, access, or disclosure is not limited to the minimum necessary.

## risk:hipaa_safeguards_gap
No administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for ePHI.

## risk:hipaa_subcontractor_gap
Subcontractors that handle PHI are not bound by equivalent BAA restrictions.

## risk:hipaa_breach_notice_gap
No prompt written Breach / Security Incident notice to the covered entity.

## risk:hipaa_return_destroy_gap
No return-or-destroy duty for PHI at termination, with an infeasibility exception.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
