Signing, witnessing, deed, or company-seal formalities.

## rule:ie.eca_eidas_esign
ECA 2000 generally permits e-signature; eIDAS applies directly and prevails in conflict. Documents requiring a seal, statutory witnessing, or an interest in real property are excluded from ordinary e-signature.

## rule:ie.companies_act_s43_seal
Company seal use requires a prior board resolution (Companies Act 2014 s.43).

## risk:ie_esign_overreach
Standard e-signature is used on a document that needs a seal, statutory witnessing, or concerns an interest in real property.

## risk:ie_seal_without_board
The company seal is applied without a prior board resolution under Companies Act 2014 s.43.
