# UK employment statutory particulars overlay (draft)

## Coverage
This is not a full employment playbook. The supplied `Employment Statutory Terms.pdf` is the UK Employment Rights (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019 (worker particulars, s.4 changes, s.11 tribunal reference). Tribunal penalty-amount changes are a public enforcement setting and are not turned into contract rules. ICE 2% consultation-threshold amendments are workplace-statute, not contract terms, and are omitted here.

## clause:statement_of_particulars
Written statement of initial employment / worker particulars and changes.

## clause:governing_law
Choice of law and/or forum.

## rule:uk.era.s1.worker_particulars
ERA 1996 s.1 statements now apply to workers, not only employees. Flag employee-only particulars templates.

## rule:uk.era.s4.changes
ERA 1996 s.4 statements of change also cover workers.

## rule:uk.era.s11.reference
A worker may refer incomplete or missing particulars under s.11. Do not treat the statement as a non-actionable courtesy document.

## risk:uk_worker_particulars_gap
Written particulars are missing or still drafted as employee-only after the 2019 worker extension.

## risk:uk_particulars_change_gap
Change-of-particulars language does not cover workers.

## risk:uk_particulars_reference_gap
The template treats particulars as optional and ignores the worker's s.11 reference route.

Other material contractual risk.
