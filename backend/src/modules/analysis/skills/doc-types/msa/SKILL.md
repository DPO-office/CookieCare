# Master services agreement skill (draft)

## Coverage
Inherits `doc-types/commercial-agreement`. Source is `MSA_AI_Prompt_Repository_Playbook.docx.pdf` (an MSA vetting/checklist playbook, not a model MSA). The file named `Master Services Agreement (MSA).pdf` is the Contract Clause Linkages Guide and is not used as an MSA primary source.

## clause:sow_hierarchy
Relationship between the MSA and statements of work, including conflict control.

## clause:acceptance
Inspection, testing, and acceptance or rejection of deliverables.

## clause:intellectual_property
Ownership and license of deliverables / work product.

## clause:limitation_of_liability
Cap or exclusion of liability between the parties.

## rule:msa.sow_hierarchy
MSA terms should govern SOWs and prevail unless the SOW expressly cites the overridden MSA section (playbook A1).

## rule:msa.acceptance_window
Flag deemed/automatic acceptance on delivery. Playbook baseline is a 14–30 day inspection window before payment (A2).

## rule:msa.work_product_ownership
Customer should own custom work product upon payment; vendor keeps background IP with a licence to use it in the deliverables (B1–B2).

## rule:msa.liability_cap_baseline
Playbook baseline: mutual cap of at least ~12 months' fees, with exceptions for IP infringement, confidentiality, and gross negligence (E1–E2).

## risk:msa_sow_hierarchy_gap
No order of precedence between the MSA and statements of work.

## risk:msa_deemed_acceptance
Deliverables are deemed accepted on delivery without an inspection window.

## risk:msa_vendor_owns_work_product
Custom deliverables remain with the vendor rather than vesting in the customer on payment.

## risk:msa_weak_liability_cap
Liability cap is unilateral or below about 12 months' fees without named exceptions.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
