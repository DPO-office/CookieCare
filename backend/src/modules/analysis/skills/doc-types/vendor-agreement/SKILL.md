# Vendor / procurement agreement skill (draft)

## Coverage
Sourced from the TIPS Vendor Agreement (cooperative purchasing). This is a procurement overlay on commercial-agreement, not a full third-party-risk playbook.

## clause:freight
Shipping, delivery, and freight pass-through mechanics.

## clause:customer_support
Order support, training, and response timing for the buying entity.

## clause:limitation_of_liability
Cap or exclusion of liability between the parties.

## rule:vendor.freight_passthrough
Show freight as a line item; pass through at cost with no markup unless alternative terms are agreed.

## rule:vendor.manufacturer_warranty
Include the manufacturer's minimum standard warranty unless a different warranty is agreed in writing; goods are new unless clearly stated.

## rule:vendor.timely_support
Provide timely, accurate customer support for orders within a commercially reasonable time.

## rule:vendor.po_parties
A PO processed through the cooperative is a contract between customer and vendor only; the cooperative is not a party to that order.

## rule:vendor.assignment_consent
Assignment of the vendor agreement requires written agreement of the parties.

## risk:vendor_freight_markup
Shipping or freight is marked up rather than passed through at cost.

## risk:vendor_warranty_gap
No manufacturer minimum warranty, or used goods are not clearly disclosed.

## risk:vendor_support_gap
No timely customer-support commitment for orders.

## risk:vendor_po_party_confusion
The purchasing cooperative is treated as a party to the customer's purchase order.

## risk:vendor_unilateral_assignment
The vendor may assign the agreement without written counterparty consent.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
