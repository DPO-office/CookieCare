# Cybersecurity and incident-response topic skill (draft)

## Coverage
NIS2 legal duties for in-scope private essential/important entities, plus NIST CSF 2.0 recommendations where the contract adopts CSF. The supplied SEC PDF is a 2026 Regulation S-K comment letter, not Item 1.05, and is not authored as a rule. Keep NIS2 clocks distinct from CSF outcomes via each rule's legalHook.

## clause:security_measures
Technical, operational, and organisational cybersecurity measures.

## clause:incident_response
Incident handling, notification, and restoration mechanics.

## rule:nis2.art21.risk_management
NIS2 Art. 21 legal duty: appropriate and proportionate measures including incident handling, supply-chain security, vulnerability handling, and continuity.

## rule:nis2.art23.incident_reporting
NIS2 Art. 23 legal duty: early warning within 24 hours of awareness, incident notification within 72 hours, and a final report. Do not contract a slower exclusive notice path.

## rule:nist.csf.govern
NIST CSF 2.0 Govern function — recommendation unless the contract incorporates CSF. Not a NIS2 legal duty.

## rule:nist.csf.detect_respond_recover
NIST CSF 2.0 Detect, Respond, Recover — recommendation unless incorporated. Distinct from NIS2 Art. 23 reporting clocks.

## risk:nis2_risk_management_gap
In-scope NIS2 cybersecurity risk-management measures are not addressed.

## risk:nis2_incident_reporting_gap
NIS2 24-hour / 72-hour incident-reporting duties are not operationalised.

## risk:nist_csf_govern_gap
CSF 2.0 Govern outcomes are not reflected where CSF is the chosen standard.

## risk:nist_csf_drr_gap
CSF 2.0 Detect, Respond, or Recover outcomes are not reflected where CSF is the chosen standard.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
