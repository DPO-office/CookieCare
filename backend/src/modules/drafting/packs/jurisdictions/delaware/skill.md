
## delaware/skill.md

### When this applies
Contract's governing law field is set to Delaware.

### Requirements
- MUST treat a Delaware choice-of-law clause as strongly enforceable — Delaware courts recognize it as a "significant, material and reasonable relationship" with the state regardless of other connections. (6 Del. C. § 2708(a))
- SHOULD pair governing law with an exclusive Delaware forum-selection clause — duly executed forum clauses are presumptively valid in Delaware. Note: enforceability of a Delaware forum clause in a court *outside* Delaware is less certain — flag as a drafting risk, not a hard rule.
- MUST NOT assume Delaware choice-of-law overrides another state's fundamental public policy on issues like non-competes — Delaware courts have limited enforcement in such conflicts.
- MUST NOT require wet-ink signature for standard commercial contracts — Delaware's UETA gives electronic signatures/records the same legal effect as originals.

### Local quirks
- No general corporate seal requirement for ordinary commercial contracts.
- If drafting a forum-selection clause, confirm whether it's meant to be exclusive — under Delaware's UETA, a forum clause is not exclusive unless the agreement expressly says so.
