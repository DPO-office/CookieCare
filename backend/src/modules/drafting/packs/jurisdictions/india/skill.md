## india/skill.md

### When this applies
Governing law is India, or the user asked for an agreement under Indian law.

### Requirements
- MUST govern the agreement by the laws of India. Do not default to England, Delaware, or Ireland because those packs exist.
- MUST identify a seat or courts for disputes if the user has specified one. If none is specified, use a bracketed seat rather than inventing a city.
- SHOULD note that personal-data processing for offering goods or services to Data Principals is addressed by the DPDPA regime pack (`regimes/dpdpa`), not by GDPR Article 28, unless the user also asked for GDPR.

### Standard governing law clause
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

### Local quirks
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 uses Data Fiduciary, Data Processor, and Data Principal. Do not substitute GDPR controller/processor labels in an India-only draft.
- Cross-border transfer of personal data is permitted except to countries or territories restricted by the Central Government (DPDPA s.16). Do not attach EU SCCs as the India transfer tool.
