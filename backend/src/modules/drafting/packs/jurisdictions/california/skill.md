## california/skill.md

### When this applies
Governing law field is set to California, OR any party is a California resident/employee (see non-compete rule below — this one travels with the party, not just the governing-law field).

### Requirements
- **MUST NOT include a non-compete or similar restraint-of-trade clause** in any contract governed by California law or involving a California party — Business & Professions Code §16600 voids virtually all such restraints, and as of 2024 this applies even if signed and performed entirely outside California. This is the single highest-stakes rule in this file — flag for mandatory human review if ACT ever drafts anything resembling a restrictive covenant in a California-touching deal.
- SHOULD apply a narrower "rule of reason" instead for purely commercial (non-employment) restrictive covenants between two businesses — California case law treats these differently from employee non-competes. Flag for lawyer judgment rather than auto-resolving.
- MUST confirm both parties have agreed to conduct the transaction electronically before relying on e-signature validity — California's UETA conditions validity on this prior mutual agreement; it isn't automatic.
