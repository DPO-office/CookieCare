# Partnership / Teaming Agreement

**When to use**: Use when two or more parties wish to collaborate on a joint project, business venture, or strategic initiative and need to document their respective roles, financial contributions, revenue sharing, and governance.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Purpose
2. Roles and Responsibilities
3. Financial Terms and Revenue Sharing
4. IP and Governance
5. Term and Dissolution
6. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify all Partners by full legal name and state the partnership commencement date.
- MUST state the strategic purpose, joint project scope, or business objective of the partnership with sufficient specificity.
- MUST define each Partner role, responsibilities, resource commitments, and contribution (financial, operational, or IP).
- MUST state the profit / revenue / cost sharing formula or percentage for each Partner.
- MUST address capital contributions and how additional capital calls are handled.
- MUST address accounting, financial reporting, and record-keeping obligations between Partners.
- MUST specify who owns background IP each Partner contributes and confirm it is not transferred to the partnership.
- MUST address joint IP created during the partnership: either jointly owned (with defined usage rights) or assigned to one Partner.
- MUST include governance provisions: decision-making authority, voting thresholds for major decisions, and deadlock resolution.
- MUST include a confidentiality covenant preventing disclosure of the other Partner commercial information.
- MUST specify the partnership term (fixed or ongoing) and renewal conditions.
- MUST include exit mechanics: notice period for voluntary exit, effect of exit on obligations, and wind-up procedures.
- MUST address the consequences of a Partner insolvency or change of control.
- MUST include a non-solicitation restriction preventing Partners from poaching each other key personnel during the term and for a reasonable post-term period.
- MUST include a limitation of liability between Partners.
- MUST state governing law and jurisdiction for disputes.
