# Service Agreement / Statement of Work

**When to use**: Use when a service provider is engaged to deliver specific professional or consulting services under a standalone agreement or statement of work (SOW). Distinct from an MSA in that this agreement governs a single engagement rather than a master relationship.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties
2. Definitions
3. Scope of Services
4. Fees
5. Term
6. Miscellaneous

**Requirements**
- MUST identify the Service Provider and Client by full legal name and state the agreement effective date.
- MUST define the scope of services with specificity sufficient to determine completion — vague descriptions create disputes.
- MUST state the fees payable, invoicing schedule, and payment window (e.g. Net 30 from invoice date).
- MUST specify whether the relationship is a fixed-fee engagement, time-and-materials, or milestone-based.
- MUST include the commencement date and completion date or project term.
- MUST include a termination-for-convenience right with reasonable notice (at least 14 days) allowing either party to exit.
- MUST include a termination-for-cause right without notice on material breach that is not cured within a reasonable cure period.
- MUST confirm that the Service Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee or partner of the Client.
- MUST address IP ownership: specify whether work product is owned by the Client (custom deliverables) or the Service Provider (background IP).
- MUST include a confidentiality obligation protecting Client non-public information for the duration of the agreement and at least 2 years post-termination.
- MUST include a limitation of liability cap, typically capped at fees paid under the agreement.
- MUST state the governing law and jurisdiction.
- SHOULD include a warranty that the services will be performed with reasonable skill and care.
- SHOULD include an invoicing dispute resolution process before payment can be withheld.
