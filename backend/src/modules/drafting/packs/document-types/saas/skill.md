# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**When to use**: Use when a SaaS Provider grants a Customer a subscription-based right to access and use a cloud-hosted software application or platform over a defined term.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Recitals
2. Definitions
3. SaaS Service and Subscription Access
4. Fees and Payment Terms
5. Customer Data and Security
6. Intellectual Property Rights
7. Service Levels and Support
8. Confidentiality
9. Warranties and Disclaimers
10. Limitation of Liability
11. Term and Termination
12. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify the SaaS Provider and Customer by full legal name and state the subscription effective date.
- MUST define Authorized Users, the SaaS service description, Customer Data, and Subscription Term as capitalised defined terms.
- MUST grant the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the service for internal business purposes only, limited to Authorized Users.
- MUST prohibit the Customer from sublicensing, reselling, reverse engineering, or creating derivative works of the service.
- MUST state subscription fees, invoicing schedule (e.g. annual in advance or monthly), and payment due date.
- MUST include late payment interest and the Provider right to suspend access on non-payment after notice.
- MUST confirm Customer Data ownership vests in the Customer; Provider has no right to use Customer Data except to deliver the service.
- MUST require Provider to implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect Customer Data.
- MUST include data breach notification obligations requiring prompt notice to Customer upon discovery of any security incident affecting Customer Data.
- MUST confirm Provider retains full ownership of the SaaS platform, software, and all underlying IP; the subscription is not a sale.
- MUST include an uptime availability commitment (e.g. 99.9% monthly) and define Scheduled Maintenance exclusions.
- MUST specify the remedy for SLA breaches, typically service credits, as the Customer sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures.
- MUST cap Provider aggregate liability to the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months immediately preceding the claim.
- MUST exclude indirect, consequential, incidental, and punitive damages for both parties.
- MUST define the initial Subscription Term and auto-renewal mechanics, including notice period to cancel renewal.
- MUST allow termination for material breach with a cure period of at least 30 days.
- MUST grant Customer a minimum 30-day data-export window after termination before Provider deletes Customer Data.
- MUST include mutual confidentiality obligations for the duration of the term and at least 3 years post-termination.
- MUST state governing law, jurisdiction, and dispute resolution venue.
- SHOULD include acceptable use policy restrictions and grounds for immediate suspension on AUP breach.

**Exhibits normally attached**
- Exhibit A: Service Description and Scope
- Exhibit B: Service Level Agreement (uptime targets and credits)
- Exhibit C: Data Processing Agreement (when Customer Data includes personal data)
