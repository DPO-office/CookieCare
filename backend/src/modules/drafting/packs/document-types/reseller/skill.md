# Reseller / Distribution Agreement

**When to use**: Use when a Supplier appoints a Reseller or Distributor to market and sell the Supplier products or services within a defined territory, on either an exclusive or non-exclusive basis.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Appointment
2. Territory and Products
3. Pricing, Margins, and Ordering
4. Term and Termination
5. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify the Supplier and Reseller by full legal name and state the agreement effective date.
- MUST specify whether the appointment is exclusive or non-exclusive within the territory.
- MUST define the authorized territory geographically with sufficient precision (country, region, or named markets).
- MUST list the products or services the Reseller is authorized to distribute; any products not listed are excluded.
- MUST state the wholesale prices or pricing methodology, discount structure, and the Reseller markup or margin framework.
- MUST define the ordering process, minimum order quantities (if any), and lead times.
- MUST require the Reseller to accurately represent the products and comply with Supplier marketing guidelines.
- MUST prohibit the Reseller from making representations about the products that exceed Supplier specifications.
- MUST include a sell-off period after termination during which the Reseller may sell remaining inventory before returning unsold stock.
- MUST specify the agreement initial term and renewal conditions.
- MUST include termination for cause on material breach with a minimum 30-day cure period.
- MUST confirm that the Reseller is an independent contractor with no authority to bind the Supplier.
- MUST include confidentiality obligations covering pricing, customer lists, and Supplier trade information.
- MUST include a limitation of liability and exclude consequential damages.
- MUST state governing law and jurisdiction.
