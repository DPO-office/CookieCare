# Software / IP License Agreement

**When to use**: Use when an IP owner (Licensor) grants another party (Licensee) a right to use software, technology, patents, trademarks, or other intellectual property under defined conditions, without transferring ownership.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Recitals
2. Grant of License
3. License Restrictions
4. Fees and Royalties
5. IP Ownership
6. Term and Termination
7. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify the Licensor and Licensee by full legal name and state the license effective date.
- MUST specify the licensed material with sufficient precision: software name and version range, patent numbers, trademark registrations, or description of the IP.
- MUST state whether the license is exclusive or non-exclusive, and if exclusive, the scope of exclusivity.
- MUST define the permitted territory (e.g. worldwide, country-specific, region-specific).
- MUST define the permitted field of use or purpose for which the Licensee may exercise the license.
- MUST state whether sublicensing is permitted and under what conditions; default is no sublicensing without written consent.
- MUST include license restrictions: no reverse engineering, no decompilation, no modification without consent, no removal of proprietary notices.
- MUST state license fees, royalty rates (if applicable), payment schedule, and currency.
- MUST include audit rights for the Licensor to verify Licensee compliance with fee and usage obligations.
- MUST confirm that the Licensor retains all title, ownership, and proprietary rights in the licensed IP; no ownership is transferred.
- MUST state the license term (fixed term or perpetual) and auto-renewal or expiry mechanics.
- MUST include termination for cause on material breach with a reasonable cure period.
- MUST require Licensee to destroy or return all copies of licensed material upon termination.
- MUST include a warranty by Licensor that it has the right to grant the license; exclude all other warranties.
- MUST limit Licensor liability to fees paid in the preceding 12 months and exclude consequential damages.
- MUST state governing law and jurisdiction.
