# General Legal Agreement

**When to use**: Use when a specific document-type pack does not match the requested agreement type. This is a general-purpose fallback that produces a structurally sound commercial agreement skeleton. Prompt the user to clarify the document type if possible.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Background
2. Definitions
3. Scope and Obligations
4. Term and Termination
5. Liability and Indemnification
6. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify all contracting parties by full legal entity name, address, and state the agreement effective date in the preamble.
- MUST include a recitals / background section explaining the commercial context for the agreement.
- MUST define all key capitalised terms used in the agreement in a Definitions section.
- MUST clearly state the core obligations, covenants, and deliverables of each party in the Scope section.
- MUST include performance standards or acceptance criteria for any deliverables where applicable.
- MUST specify the agreement initial term, renewal conditions, and termination rights (for cause and for convenience).
- MUST include a limitation of liability cap — typically the fees paid or a fixed monetary amount.
- MUST exclude indirect, consequential, incidental, and punitive damages from each party liability.
- MUST state which party will indemnify the other for third-party claims and on what terms.
- MUST include a governing law and jurisdiction clause.
- MUST include standard boilerplate: entire agreement, severability, no waiver, amendments in writing, and notices.
- SHOULD include a confidentiality obligation if either party will receive non-public information from the other.
- Do NOT include a Table of Contents, preamble headers listing section numbers, or any TOC-style section.
