# Employment Agreement

**When to use**: Use when an employer is hiring an employee and wishes to document the terms of employment, including role, compensation, confidentiality, IP assignment, restrictive covenants, and termination.

**Standard section list**
1. Parties and Employment Offer
2. Position, Role, and Duties
3. Compensation and Benefits
4. Confidentiality and IP Assignment
5. Restrictive Covenants
6. Termination of Employment
7. Miscellaneous and Governing Law

**Requirements**
- MUST identify the Employer by full legal name and the Employee by full legal name and state the employment start date.
- MUST specify the employee job title, reporting structure, and core job duties or responsibilities.
- MUST state the base salary or hourly rate, payment frequency, and any probationary period.
- MUST disclose or describe any bonus, commission, or incentive plans that apply during the employment.
- MUST describe the main employee benefits (health insurance, pension, paid leave) without creating additional contractual obligations beyond the stated terms.
- MUST include a confidentiality covenant prohibiting disclosure of Employer trade secrets and proprietary information both during and after employment.
- MUST assign all inventions, works, and IP created by the Employee in the course of employment to the Employer as a work-for-hire or by assignment.
- MUST specify the notice period required for resignation or termination, which must comply with the minimum statutory period under applicable law.
- MUST state the grounds for summary dismissal (gross misconduct, material breach) without requiring notice.
- MUST include a non-solicitation restriction preventing the Employee from soliciting Employer clients or employees for a reasonable post-employment period.
- MUST set the governing law to the jurisdiction of the employment, with courts of that jurisdiction having exclusive jurisdiction.
- MUST include standard boilerplate: entire agreement, severability, amendments in writing.
- SHOULD include a probationary period with a shorter notice period during probation.
- SHOULD cap any non-compete duration at 12 months maximum and limit geographic scope to areas where the Employee actively worked.

**Exhibits normally attached**
- Exhibit A: Job Description (optional)
- Exhibit B: Bonus / Commission Structure (if applicable)
