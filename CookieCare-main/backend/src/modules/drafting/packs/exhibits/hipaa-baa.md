## 1. Definitions

Terms used in this Annex have the meanings given in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (the HIPAA Rules).

## 2. Obligations of Business Associate

(a) Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Annex or as required by law.

(b) Business Associate shall use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 with respect to electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this Annex.

(c) Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Annex of which it becomes aware, including breaches of unsecured PHI as required at 45 CFR 164.410.

(d) Business Associate shall ensure that any subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of Business Associate agree to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to Business Associate.

(e) Business Associate shall make available PHI in accordance with 45 CFR 164.524, and make available PHI for amendment and incorporate amendments as required by 45 CFR 164.526.

(f) Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records available to the Secretary of HHS for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

(g) Upon termination of this Annex, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI, if feasible; if not feasible, extend the protections of this Annex to the retained PHI.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as necessary to perform services under the principal agreement, and as required by law, subject to the minimum necessary standard.

## 4. Termination

Covered Entity may terminate this Annex for material breach if Business Associate fails to cure within a reasonable period after written notice.
