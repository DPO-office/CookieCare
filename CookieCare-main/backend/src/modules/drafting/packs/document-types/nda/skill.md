# nda.md

**When to use this document type**
Use when confidential, proprietary, or trade secret information is shared between parties for evaluating a potential commercial relationship or transaction[cite: 4].

**Standard section list**
1. Definition of Confidential Information[cite: 4]
2. Purpose & Permitted Use[cite: 4]
3. Standard Exceptions[cite: 4]
4. Compelled Disclosure[cite: 4]
5. Term & Survival[cite: 4]
6. Return or Destruction[cite: 4]
7. Remedies & Injunctive Relief[cite: 4]
8. No License or Obligation[cite: 4]
9. Non-Solicitation[cite: 4]
10. Miscellaneous[cite: 4]

**Requirements**
- MUST structure the agreement as bilateral/mutual whenever both parties are sharing confidential information[cite: 4].
- MUST strictly restrict the use of Confidential Information to the explicitly stated Business Purpose[cite: 4].
- MUST include broad coverage for technical, financial, and operational data without strict oral marking traps[cite: 4].
- MUST include standard carve-outs for public domain, prior knowledge, independent development, and third-party receipt[cite: 4].
- MUST permit legally compelled disclosures subject to prompt advance written notice where legally permissible[cite: 4].
- MUST cap general confidentiality obligations at 2 to 3 years maximum[cite: 4].
- MUST require perpetual or indefinite confidentiality protection for Trade Secrets for as long as protected under applicable law[cite: 4].
- MUST require return or certified destruction of confidential information within 30 days of written request[cite: 4].
- MUST include explicit exceptions allowing retention of a single legal archival copy and standard automated system backups[cite: 4].
- MUST explicitly state that disclosure grants no patent or IP licenses and assigns no residual rights[cite: 4].
- MUST clarify that execution of the NDA does not compel either party to enter into a future commercial transaction[cite: 4].
- MUST set governing law and jurisdiction to an acceptable corporate venue[cite: 4].
- SHOULD cap non-solicitation of employees at 12 months maximum with an explicit carve-out for general hiring advertisements[cite: 4].
- SHOULD provide balanced injunctive relief language without waiving bond requirements or making automatic admissions of irreparable harm[cite: 4].

**Exhibits normally attached**
- Exhibit A: Description of Stated Business Purpose (optional)[cite: 4]
