# msa.md

**When to use this document type**
Use when establishing an overarching commercial relationship with a vendor or customer that sets standard legal and operational defaults for future Statements of Work (SOWs)[cite: 5].

**Standard section list**
1. Scope & Relationship to SOWs[cite: 5]
2. Performance of Services & Deliverable Acceptance[cite: 5]
3. Fees, Invoicing & Payment[cite: 5]
4. Intellectual Property Rights[cite: 5]
5. Confidentiality & Data Rights[cite: 5]
6. Warranties & SLA References[cite: 5]
7. Indemnification[cite: 5]
8. Limitation of Liability[cite: 5]
9. Term & Termination[cite: 5]
10. Miscellaneous[cite: 5]

**Requirements**
- MUST establish that the MSA governs all SOWs and supersedes conflicting SOW terms unless the SOW explicitly cites the superseded MSA section[cite: 5].
- MUST include a minimum 14-to-30-day explicit acceptance testing and inspection window for deliverables prior to payment obligations[cite: 5].
- MUST require prior written notice before replacing key team members or subcontracting services[cite: 5].
- MUST assign 100% ownership of custom deliverables and Work Product to the Customer upon payment[cite: 5].
- MUST grant the Customer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use pre-existing background IP embedded in deliverables[cite: 5].
- MUST ensure the Customer retains sole ownership of Customer Data bound by strict Vendor non-use restrictions[cite: 5].
- MUST set payment terms to a minimum of Net 30 or Net 45 days after receipt of a valid invoice[cite: 5].
- MUST grant the right to withhold payment on good-faith disputed amounts without late interest penalties or service pauses[cite: 5].
- MUST cap annual price increases at CPI or a maximum of 3% to 5% with at least 60 days advance written notice[cite: 5].
- MUST require the Vendor to defend and indemnify the Customer against third-party IP infringement claims on an uncapped basis[cite: 5].
- MUST include a minimum 90-day warranty that services and software conform to specifications[cite: 5].
- MUST set mutual general liability caps at a minimum equal to 12 months of fees (1x ACV)[cite: 5].
- MUST establish uncapped or super-capped exceptions to the liability cap for IP infringement, confidentiality breaches, and gross negligence[cite: 5].
- MUST grant the Customer the right to terminate for convenience with 30 to 60 days written notice[cite: 5].
- MUST require a 30-day cure period prior to termination for material breach[cite: 5].
- SHOULD include mutual confidentiality obligations surviving 3 years for standard information and perpetual protection for trade secrets[cite: 5].
