
## england/skill.md

### When this applies
Governing law field is set to England, England and Wales, or UK (confirm which — see quirk below).

### Requirements
- MUST classify the document as a "simple contract" or a "deed" before drafting the execution block — most commercial agreements (NDA, MSA, DPA, SLA) are simple contracts: signature by an authorized person plus consideration is sufficient. Deeds (e.g. certain releases, guarantees, assignments without consideration) need stricter formalities.
- MUST execute company-signed deeds per Companies Act 2006 s.44: two directors, a director and company secretary, or one director in the presence of a witness.
- MUST NOT rely on remote/video witnessing for a deed — even where e-signatures are used, the witness must be physically present with the signatory.
- SHOULD require only one authorized signatory (no witness) for simple contracts.

### Local quirks

- Companies without a company secretary should use the single-director-plus-witness execution method.
