# HIPAA Business Associate skill

## Applicability
Applies when PHI is involved (`phiInvolved` true) or the deal context involves patient/health/medical/HIPAA data.

## Required facts
- Confirm whether **PHI is in scope**. If skill applicability is ambiguous from free text but health data is hinted, ask phiInvolved critically — BA obligations change the entire security and breach sections.

## Mandatory requirements
- **Permitted uses and disclosures of PHI** must be limited and specified.
- The BA must implement HIPAA **administrative, physical, and technical safeguards**.
- **Breach notification to the covered entity** is required under the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule as applied to business associates.

## Section mapping hints
- BA-specific obligations → sec-hipaa-ba
- Breach notification → sec-breach
