# EU AI Act skill (draft)

## Coverage
Private provider, deployer, importer, and distributor duties and affected-person rights under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Market-surveillance, notified-body, and public-authority institutional duties are excluded.

## clause:ai_system_use
Contractual allocation of AI provider, deployer, importer, or distributor roles and duties.

## rule:aiact.art5.prohibited
Do not contract for an Article 5 prohibited AI practice (private operators).

## rule:aiact.art16.provider
High-risk AI providers must meet Section 2 requirements plus QMS / EU declaration / CE marking before placing on the market.

## rule:aiact.art23.importer
Importers must verify technical documentation, CE marking, and authorised-representative appointment before Union placing on the market.

## rule:aiact.art24.distributor
Distributors must verify CE marking, the EU declaration, and instructions for use, and must not make a non-conforming high-risk system available.

## rule:aiact.art26.deployer
Deployers must follow instructions, assign human oversight, monitor, keep logs, and complete an FRIA where Article 27 applies. Public-authority market surveillance is out of scope.

## rule:aiact.art50.transparency
Meet Article 50 transparency duties so affected persons know they are interacting with AI or that content is AI-generated, unless an exemption applies.

## rule:aiact.art86.explanation
Do not waive or obstruct an affected person's Article 86 right to an explanation of a high-risk AI decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

## risk:aiact_prohibited_practice
The contract would allow an Article 5 prohibited AI practice.

## risk:aiact_provider_duty_gap
Provider obligations for a high-risk AI system are incomplete.

## risk:aiact_importer_duty_gap
Importer verification duties before Union placing on the market are missing.

## risk:aiact_distributor_duty_gap
Distributor CE / conformity verification duties are missing.

## risk:aiact_deployer_duty_gap
Deployer instructions, human oversight, logging, or FRIA duties are missing.

## risk:aiact_transparency_gap
Article 50 transparency to affected persons is not addressed.

## risk:aiact_explanation_right_gap
The contract waives or obstructs the Article 86 explanation right.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
