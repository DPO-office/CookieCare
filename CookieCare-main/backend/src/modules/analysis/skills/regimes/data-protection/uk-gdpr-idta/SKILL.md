# UK GDPR / IDTA skill (draft)

## Coverage
UK operational overlay: IDTA / UK Addendum, UK representative, and ICO as Commissioner. Primary legal text is the consolidated UK GDPR / DPA 2018 plus the drafting `uk-gdpr-idta` pack. The file `GDPR UK.pdf` is only S.I. 2023/1417 and is not treated as the UK GDPR. This pack does not recreate EU GDPR Articles 1–99.

## clause:data_protection
Core processing subject-matter, roles, and processor obligations annex.

## clause:international_transfer_mechanism
Mechanism for cross-border transfers (structural).

## rule:ukgdpr.idta_or_addendum
Where UK restricted transfers apply, address the UK IDTA or the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs. EU SCCs alone are not a completed UK tool.

## rule:ukgdpr.art27_uk_representative
Non-UK controllers/processors in scope of UK GDPR should designate a UK representative, distinct from any EU Article 27 representative.

## rule:ukgdpr.ico_as_authority
Complaints, DPO contacts, and UK breach notification should include the Information Commissioner, not only an EU lead authority.

## rule:ukgdpr.art46_uk_mechanism
UK Article 46 safeguards are UK-recognised tools (IDTA, UK Addendum, UK-approved BCRs, or another UK Chapter V mechanism).

## risk:uk_idta_missing
UK restricted transfers are not tied to the IDTA or UK Addendum.

## risk:uk_representative_gap
No UK representative is designated where UK extra-territorial processing applies.

## risk:uk_ico_authority_gap
The document points only to an EU authority and omits the Information Commissioner.

## risk:uk_transfer_safeguard_gap
UK transfers rely on unmodified EU SCCs without a UK-recognised safeguard.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
