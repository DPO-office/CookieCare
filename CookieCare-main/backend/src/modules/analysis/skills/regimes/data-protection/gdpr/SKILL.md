# GDPR Article 28 / Chapter III skill

## Scope
GDPR-specific processor obligations and Chapter III data-subject rights assistance (Arts 15–22)
plus the Art 12(3) response timeframe. Doc-shape DPA checks are in `doc-types/dpa`.

## rule:gdpr.art28.3.a
Documented instructions-only processing, including regarding transfers.

## rule:gdpr.art28.3.b
Persons authorised to process personal data are under confidentiality obligations.

## rule:gdpr.art28.3.e
Processor assists the controller with Chapter III data-subject rights by appropriate TOMs.
EDPB Guidelines 07/2020, para 121 — generic DSR language may meet the spirit but not the letter.

## rule:gdpr.art28.3.h
Information and audit assistance to demonstrate compliance.

## rule:gdpr.art12.3
One-month response timeframe (extendable +2 months). Controller remains liable under Art 12(3) / Art 83(5)(b).

## clause:data_subject_request_handling
How the processor handles data-subject requests — prefer naming Arts 15–22.

## clause:processor_assistance_obligation
Processor duty to assist the controller with fulfilment of Chapter III rights.

## clause:automated_decision_disclosure
Art 22 automated decision-making disclosure / human review.

## risk:dsr_generic_no_named_rights
DSR / assistance clause exists but does not name specific Chapter III rights (Arts 15–22).

## risk:dsr_no_response_timeframe
No numeric response timeframe tied to Art 12(3); "promptly" or "reasonably" alone is a gap.

## risk:erasure_termination_only_gap
Deletion is only on termination; no mid-term Art 17 erasure path.

## risk:portability_format_unaddressed
No structured / machine-readable export commitment for Art 20.

## risk:automated_decision_gap
Art 22 automated decision-making is unaddressed.

## risk:recipient_notification_gap
Art 19 notification to recipients / subprocessor flow-down is unaddressed.

## risk:assistance_cost_or_consent_gate_risk
Assistance is gated on cost, consent, or controller instruction in a way that may blow Art 12(3).

## risk:missing_carve_out
Required GDPR carve-outs from liability caps may be absent.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
