# California jurisdiction skill (draft)

## Coverage
Deepened from the drafting California pack: §16600 employee non-competes, commercial vs employment restraints, and UETA mutual e-consent. Not a full California employment code.

## clause:governing_law
Choice of law and/or forum.

## clause:non_compete
Post-termination non-compete / restrictive covenant.

## clause:electronic_signature
Electronic signature validity and any required prior consent to transact electronically.

## rule:ca.ueta_mutual_consent
California UETA conditions e-signature validity on prior mutual agreement to transact electronically. Prefer an express e-consent clause.

## rule:ca.commercial_vs_employment_restraint
Employee non-competes touching California are generally void under §16600. Purely commercial restraints between businesses use a narrower rule of reason — flag for lawyer judgment rather than auto-voiding.

## risk:ca_non_compete_unenforceable
California generally voids employee non-competes (Bus. & Prof. Code §16600); flag as likely unenforceable.

## risk:ca_ueta_consent_missing
Electronic execution is relied on without prior mutual agreement to transact electronically under California UETA.
