# Delaware jurisdiction skill (draft)

## Coverage
Deepened from the drafting Delaware pack: 6 Del. C. §2708, forum exclusivity, public-policy non-compete limit, and UETA. Not a full Delaware corporate code.

## clause:governing_law
Choice of law and/or forum.

## clause:non_compete
Post-termination non-compete / restrictive covenant.

## clause:electronic_signature
Electronic signature validity and any required prior consent to transact electronically.

## rule:de.choice_of_law_2708
Treat a Delaware choice-of-law clause as strongly enforceable under 6 Del. C. §2708(a) (significant, material, and reasonable relationship).

## rule:de.forum_exclusivity
If exclusive Delaware courts are intended, say so expressly. A forum clause is not exclusive unless the agreement says so. Enforceability outside Delaware is less certain.

## rule:de.public_policy_noncompete
Do not assume Delaware choice-of-law overrides another state's fundamental public policy on non-competes.

## rule:de.ueta_esign
Delaware UETA gives electronic signatures the same effect as originals. Do not require wet-ink for a standard commercial contract. No general corporate-seal requirement.

## risk:de_choice_of_law_weak
The Delaware governing-law clause is missing or does not sit cleanly with 6 Del. C. §2708.

## risk:de_forum_not_exclusive
Forum selection is not expressly exclusive, or exclusivity may fail outside Delaware.

## risk:de_public_policy_noncompete
Delaware choice-of-law is used as if it automatically overrides another state's fundamental non-compete public policy.

## risk:de_wet_ink_required
The contract requires wet-ink execution for a standard commercial contract governed by Delaware law.

Other material contractual risk.
