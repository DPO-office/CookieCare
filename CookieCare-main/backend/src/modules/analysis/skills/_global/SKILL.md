# General contract review skill (`_global`)

## Scope
Broad commercial contract review: liability, indemnity, termination, governing law, and cross-cutting obligations.
Always active as the global baseline; specialized doc-type and regime skills compose on top.

## clause:limitation_of_liability
Mutual limitation of liability with carve-outs for fraud, wilful misconduct, and confidentiality breaches.

## clause:indemnity
Balanced indemnity scoped to third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's breach.

## clause:termination
Clear termination for cause and convenience with symmetric notice periods where appropriate.

## clause:governing_law
Governing law and forum should be explicit and consistent with the commercial relationship.

## risk:uncapped_liability
Unlimited or asymmetric liability exposure.

## risk:one_sided_indemnity
One-sided indemnity without caps or scope limits.

## risk:unilateral_termination
Termination rights that allow one party to exit without comparable rights for the other.

## risk:ambiguous_definition
Defined terms or obligations are vague or unmeasurable.

## risk:missing_limitation_of_liability
No limitation of liability clause identified.

## risk:missing_indemnity
No indemnity clause identified where expected.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
