# Vendor risk and diligence topic skill (draft)

## Coverage
EBA/GL/2019/02 outsourcing guidelines (pre-outsourcing analysis, audit/access, exit, sub-outsourcing) plus NIST SP 800-161 C-SCRM recommendations. This is not a MiFID II / PSD2 / FCA regime pack.

## clause:outsourcing_governance
Pre-outsourcing assessment, register, and ongoing outsourcing governance.

## clause:audit_rights
Access, information, and audit rights over the service provider.

## clause:exit_strategy
Termination assistance and reintegration or transfer of an outsourced function.

## clause:subprocessor_flow_down
Subprocessor list / flow-down of processor obligations.

## rule:eba.outsourcing.pre_assessment
Complete a pre-outsourcing analysis before outsourcing a critical or important function (EBA/GL/2019/02 §12).

## rule:eba.outsourcing.audit_access
Preserve institution and competent-authority access, information, and audit rights, including into sub-outsourced locations (§13.3).

## rule:eba.outsourcing.exit
Document an exit strategy and termination assistance so a critical function can be reintegrated or transferred.

## rule:eba.outsourcing.suboutsourcing
Control sub-outsourcing of critical or important functions with notice/consent and equivalent flow-down (§13.1).

## rule:nist.800161.cscm
Where NIST SP 800-161 is chosen, look for supplier assessment, security flow-down, and ongoing monitoring. Recommendation unless incorporated by contract.

## risk:eba_pre_outsourcing_gap
No pre-outsourcing analysis for a critical or important function.

## risk:eba_audit_access_gap
The institution or competent authority lacks access, information, or audit rights.

## risk:eba_exit_strategy_gap
No documented exit strategy or termination assistance for critical outsourcing.

## risk:eba_suboutsourcing_gap
Sub-outsourcing of critical or important functions lacks notice, consent, or flow-down.

## risk:nist_cscm_gap
NIST SP 800-161 supply-chain risk practices are not reflected where that standard is chosen.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
