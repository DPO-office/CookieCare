# Commercial agreements skill

## Scope
NDA, MSA, SLA, and general commercial contracts: payment, IP, confidentiality, renewal, and service levels.

## What good looks like
- Clear payment terms, invoice dispute process, and IP ownership of deliverables.
- Mutual confidentiality with defined survival period.
- SLA metrics with measurable remedies and exclusions documented.

## Red flags
- Auto-renewal without adequate opt-out notice.
- Broad IP assignment beyond project deliverables.
- One-sided service credits or SLA exclusions.
