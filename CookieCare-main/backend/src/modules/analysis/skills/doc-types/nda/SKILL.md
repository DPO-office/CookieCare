# Non-disclosure agreement skill (draft)

## Coverage
Structural mutual-NDA checks plus an NLRB confidentiality overlay. The file labelled `Standard Mutual NDA Blueprint.pdf` is an ABA article on employer confidentiality rules under Boeing / Section 7, not a model NDA. Residual-knowledge, non-solicit, and term/survival playbook language is not fully sourced.

## clause:confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations and survival.

## clause:governing_law
Choice of law and/or forum.

## rule:nda.ci_definition
Define confidential information and standard exclusions (public, independent development, rightful third-party receipt). Flag an unbounded secrecy duty.

## rule:nda.purpose_limitation
Limit use to the disclosed evaluation, negotiation, or project purpose.

## rule:nda.return_or_destruction
Require return or destruction on request or when the purpose ends.

## rule:nda.nlra_section_7_carveout
For employee- or worker-facing NDAs, flag confidentiality language that would reasonably prohibit discussing wages or working conditions. Source: NLRB Boeing analysis in the supplied ABA article — not a substitute for a full mutual-NDA playbook.

## risk:nda_definition_gap
Confidential information is undefined or unbounded.

## risk:nda_purpose_limitation_gap
Confidential information may be used beyond the stated purpose.

## risk:nda_return_destruction_gap
No return or destruction obligation on expiry or request.

## risk:nda_nlra_section_7_risk
An employee-facing confidentiality rule may reasonably be read to restrict NLRA Section 7 activity.

## risk:weak_confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations are weak or one-sided.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
