# Commercial agreements skill

## Scope
NDA, MSA, SLA, and general commercial contracts: payment, IP, confidentiality, renewal, and service levels.

## clause:payment
Clear payment terms, invoice dispute process, and fee structure.

## clause:intellectual_property
IP ownership of deliverables is explicit; assignment scope is limited to project work product.

## clause:confidentiality
Mutual confidentiality with defined survival period.

## clause:limitation_of_liability
Liability caps are present and commercially balanced.

## clause:indemnity
Indemnity scope is defined and typically reviewed with the liability cap.

## risk:one_sided_indemnity
Indemnity obligations fall disproportionately on one party.

## risk:uncapped_liability
Liability is unlimited or effectively uncapped.

## risk:auto_renewal_trap
Auto-renewal without adequate opt-out notice.

## risk:broad_indemnity
Indemnity scope is unusually broad.

## risk:weak_confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations are weak or one-sided.

## risk:missing_limitation_of_liability
No limitation of liability clause identified.

## risk:other_known_risk
Other material contractual risk.
