## Findings requiring attention

### Art 47 Binding Corporate Rules

Status: Gap

Evidence:

No matching provision found in reviewed scope

Assessment: The contract fails to establish legally binding corporate rules, enforceable data subject rights, or mandatory internal privacy structure requirements across corporate group members.

Recommended action: Add or clarify contract language establishing: Binding corporate rules used as an Article 46 safeguard are legally binding on and enforceable against every relevant group member and employee, confer enforceable data-subject rights, and contain the mandatory Article 47(2) programme elements including structure, transfer details, principles, security, onward transfers, subject rights, EU-entity liability, complaints, audits, and authority cooperation.

### Art 48 Foreign Disclosure Orders

Status: Gap

Evidence:

No matching provision found in reviewed scope

Assessment: The documentation lacks provisions restricting non European Union administrative or judicial disclosure demands to recognized international agreements.

Recommended action: Add or clarify contract language establishing: A non-EU court or administrative disclosure order is enforceable as a transfer basis only when grounded in an applicable international agreement, without prejudice to another valid Chapter V transfer ground.

### Art 49 Transfer Derogations

Status: Gap

Evidence:

No matching provision found in reviewed scope

Assessment: The contract fails to define specific statutory derogation grounds, explicit consent terms, or documented assessment rules for exceptional data transfers.

Recommended action: Add or clarify contract language establishing: An Article 49 derogation is used only when its exact conditions are met — including explicit informed consent, contract necessity, legal claims, vital interests, or limited public-register access — and the narrow compelling-legitimate-interest route requires a non-repetitive limited transfer, documented circumstances and risk assessment, suitable safeguards, and required data-subject notice.
